# Product Intelligence

## Product Canon

Products are first-class, versioned entities. Intake ("I built something")
creates a `product` plus an initial `product_version` snapshot; every update
("Offer this to agencies", "Change target pricing to $5,000") writes a new
version — history is never overwritten.

The canon persists: name, codename, definition, description, core problem,
core insight, capabilities, features, technical/creative differentiators,
value propositions, customer outcomes, maturity, evidence, limitations, risks,
use cases, target customers, buyers, partners, industries, commercial models,
pricing hypotheses, competitive alternatives, positioning, delivery
requirements, founder involvement, assets, objections, FAQ, roadmap, and
revenue opportunities.

Every claim is tagged: `FOUNDER_FACT`, `VERIFIED_EVIDENCE`,
`AGENT_INTERPRETATION`, `COMMERCIAL_HYPOTHESIS`, or `ASPIRATION`.

## Capability Canon

`capability` records what 11vatedTech can genuinely deliver. Capabilities enter
from founder input, verified product/portfolio evidence, or verified project
implementation. Agent-discovered capabilities remain `hypothesis` until
founder-approved; external outreach may promise only approved capabilities.

## Product → Market engine

`market`, `market_hypothesis`, and `ideal_customer_profile` capture who should
care, why, the buyer vs user, economic value hypothesis, alternatives, switching
reasons, proof required, objections, sales motion, pricing/partnership model,
and validation plan. Unvalidated conclusions remain hypotheses.
