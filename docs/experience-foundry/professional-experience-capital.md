# Professional Experience Capital

Experience is a first-class Foundry asset.

Installed tools and written rubrics do not prove mastery. Professional capability grows through research, controlled exercises, critique, production failure, repair, independent review, human evidence, and retained lessons.

## Distinctions

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| KNOWLEDGE | understands concepts and vocabulary |
| TOOL_ACCESS | can invoke software or API |
| OPERATIONAL_SKILL | can complete bounded task on real artifacts |
| PRODUCTION_EXPERIENCE | has used skill inside realistic product constraints |
| CRITICAL_EXPERIENCE | has failed, diagnosed, repaired, and retained lesson |
| MASTERED_CAPABILITY | produces consistently strong results across materially different contexts under independent review |

## Experience Foundry loop

DISCOVER_DISCIPLINE → RESEARCH_PROFESSIONAL_PRACTICE → IDENTIFY_TOOLS → IDENTIFY_EXEMPLARS → STUDY_WORKFLOWS → PERFORM_CONTROLLED_EXERCISE → PRODUCE_ARTIFACT → REVIEW → FAIL_DIAGNOSE → REPAIR → REPEAT → APPLY_IN_REAL_PRODUCT → INDEPENDENT_REVIEW → HUMAN_REVIEW → DISTILL_LESSONS → CREATE_GOLDEN_TASK → RETAIN_EXPERIENCE.

Experience gained:

| Profile | Experience |
|---|---|
| Environment Artist | Compared three environment theses; mapped architecture/material/storytelling/set dressing to mechanic; learned physical reliquary support grammar; separated architecture, functional props, story props, scatter; documented repetition and material-causality tests. |
| Level Designer | Built equivalent three-objective blockout logic; structured player start, landmark, tutorial, route choice, recovery, climax, and reward for three different spatial models; defined no-HUD and screenshot-read tests. |
| Lighting Specialist | Defined motivated lighting sources, value hierarchy, emissive caveats, Lumen geometry constraints, atmosphere readability gates, and six controlled lighting variants. |
| Technical Artist | Produced UE 5.8 packaged runtime with command-line selectable blockouts, embedded reliquaries, lighting/material/state labs, and capture automation. |
| Game Director | Preserved tradeoff reasoning: immediate vertical-slice feasibility, emotional attachment, long-term world image, without averaging into vanity score. |

What worked:

- Durable concept evidence moved into versionable evidence hierarchy.
- Cinderworks, Hospice, and Orchard each received complete VISDEV thesis.
- Runtime package and capture evidence passed for all three directions in success and failure modes.
- Each blockout contains three reliquaries plus lighting/material/state lab proxies.
- Blind Founder packet prepared without revealing initial preference.
- Six-role specialist synthesis completed without averaging into a final winner.

What failed / remains unproven:

- Human no-HUD navigation comprehension not yet collected.
- Founder blind review synthesis pending completion.
- Founder human evidence pending.
- Lighting variants exist as lab proxies, not final judged captures.
- Material studies are proxy blocks, not shader/material response proof.
- VFX/audio state-feedback lab remains conceptual/proxy, not final implementation.
- Performance after real assets, fog, PCG, Nanite, and final materials unproven.

Generalizable lessons:

- A preferred concept must be demoted to hypothesis until tested against materially different alternatives.
- Environment direction selection needs separate evidence for mechanic support, emotional identity, world coherence, readability, and feasibility.
- Blockouts can and should encode state-feedback labs before final art.
- Color-coded state is weak evidence unless grayscale/motion/audio/spatial cues also work.
- Runtime screenshots prove capture pipeline, not human-facing visual quality.

Non-generalizable lessons:

- Cinderworks' modular feasibility depends on Ashwake's current three-reliquary mechanic and may not transfer to open-world Ashwake.
- Hospice's emotional strength depends on players accepting reliquaries as living/patient-like objects.
- Orchard's world-image strength depends on outdoor readability and production budget that this proxy pass has not proven.

Golden tasks created from failures:

1. Identify test-chamber environment: reject scenes with floating relics, no former use, no route hierarchy, no environmental state response.
2. Identify environment with no visual hierarchy: fail if focal, route, hazard, and background share value/contrast/detail weight.
3. Identify black-void lighting: fail if dark mood hides floor edges, landmarks, or interaction supports.
4. Identify attractive space that fails gameplay navigation: fail if screenshot looks strong but no-HUD player cannot state goal/path.
5. Identify state feedback dependent only on HUD or hue: fail if SAFE/HOSTILE/SUCCESS/FAILURE collapse in grayscale or muted review.
6. Identify generic cathedral/hospital/orchard drift: fail if thesis needs title text to distinguish it from common genre references.
7. Identify unsupported reliquary placement: fail if relic reads as floating display object rather than socketed/cradled/rooted infrastructure.
8. Identify particle spam masquerading as atmosphere: fail if VFX reduces route, landmark, or state readability.

Retention rule: Phase 2 cannot become final art selection until Founder evidence, human no-HUD navigation comprehension, representative walkthroughs, grayscale/state readability proof, material lookdev, and representative performance evidence are appended here.
