## Option A synthesis — sacred industrial sanctuary

### Strengths

- Best immediate blockout-to-vertical-slice path.
- Strongest modular kit candidate: ribs, flues, vault channels, brass gates, duct paths, heat seams.
- Best level-design control: spawn balcony, first objective, route choice, recovery/balance space, furnace apse climax.
- Heat circulation maps cleanly to timing windows, safe/hostile state, and success response.
- Technical risk lowest among three: contained interiors, predictable sightlines, controllable Lumen/fog/capture conditions.

### Blockers

- Generic risk severe: dark cathedral, lava cracks, forge altar, steampunk brass, Unreal lava temple.
- Black-crush risk can hide routes, interactables, and surface detail.
- Heat/cable/duct logic must read as operating civic-monastic infrastructure, not decorative pipe dressing.
- Emotional memory weaker than B unless reliquary restoration feels like more than restarting generator nodes.
- Reward state must visibly transform world, not only open a door or play emissive pulse.

### Required disproof tests

1. Remove arches. If identity dies, A was cathedral costume, not sacred industrial infrastructure.
2. Desaturate orange/gold. If route/state collapses, A relies on lava color coding.
3. Five-second grayscale route test from spawn balcony.
4. Machine-read test: blind viewer must infer heat circulation, service loop, or maintenance rite from geometry.
5. 20-meter modular repetition test with no obvious wall/floor cadence.
6. Lumen/emissive seam test across module joins with camera motion and no flicker/bloom smear.
7. Furnace-breath agency test: climax must require timing, position, or decision, not passive spectacle.

## Option B synthesis — sacred quarantine hospice

### Strengths

- Strongest emotional and environmental storytelling candidate.
- Reliquary-as-patient solves floating-relic weakness through cradles, supports, tubes, clamps, triage tags, masks, coal lungs.
- Best physical state grammar if breath, pulse, pressure, gauges, clamps, and tube brightness carry SAFE/HOSTILE without HUD.
- Richest material and set-dressing space: amber glass, waxed cloth, tarnished instruments, stained linen, coal-black lungs, tags, old ceramic.
- Failure can feel personal: attunement as care, not activation.

### Blockers

- High derivative risk: sci-fi med bay, hospital horror, plague doctor cliché, containment lab.
- Asset burden high: bespoke props, subtle animations, glass/translucency handling, tube routing, readable timing sync.
- Route clarity risk: glass partitions, ward repetition, observation rooms, and procedural corridor logic can confuse access.
- Moral tone needs control. Reliquary-as-patient can become exploitative or melodramatic if too literal.
- Choice design weakest by default; hospice path can feel like linear processing unless triage/contamination routes create meaningful agency.

### Required disproof tests

1. Remove gore, hospital signage, and modern clinic props. If care/quarantine/sacred hospice still reads, identity holds.
2. Glass-route test: players must not try blocked partitions as paths.
3. Hide gauges. SAFE/HOSTILE must still read through breath, clamps, tube flow, lung motion, and sound.
4. Animation sync test over three minutes: lung, tube pulse, gauge needles, pressure hiss cannot drift.
5. One screenshot without labels or characters must communicate care/ward/hospice, not boiler room or med bay.
6. Warning-spam test: no more than three priority signals active per view.
7. Agency test: player must act meaningfully within first minute, not ride a medical conveyor belt.

## Option C synthesis — dead fire ecology

### Strengths

- Strongest signature, visual identity, cosmology, and long-term franchise/world-image promise.
- Best escape from test-chamber feeling: ridge, cairn/shortcut, causeway, observatory climb, solar colossus, meridian gate.
- Strongest mythic transformation: roots converge, pollen flows, shadows align, false dawn opens.
- Lighting/atmosphere potential largest: eclipse rim, ash veil, ember pollen, false dawn band, long shadows.
- Can make Ashwake feel like dead fire ecology rather than room puzzle sequence.

### Blockers

- Highest proof burden: terrain, PCG/foliage, fog, particles, outdoor Lumen, LOD/horizon, overdraw, wide-shot repetition.
- Player role can blur: gardener, arsonist, prophet, engineer, or seed-bearer.
- Ecology state language more abstract; causality must be visible before/after, not poetic only.
- Vista can overpower gameplay affordances and reliquary objectives.
- Golden tree / magical forest / generic eclipse fantasy risk severe.

### Required disproof tests

1. Flat-gray blockout plus three lighting states. If path/readability fails, vista is hiding bad level design.
2. Crop horizon out. Foreground must still show route, landmark, and reliquary affordance.
3. Disable particles. If image or state collapses, ember pollen is carrying too much.
4. Objective hierarchy test: player must rank colossus/gate above sun tree/cairn/observatory without labels.
5. Representative density packaged runtime test: terrain, roots, pollen, fog, reliquaries at intended vista density.
6. GPU overdraw/fog/foliage/Lumen test on target hardware.
7. State-causality test: roots, pollen, shadow, and warmth must agree; no conflicting SAFE/HOSTILE signals.
