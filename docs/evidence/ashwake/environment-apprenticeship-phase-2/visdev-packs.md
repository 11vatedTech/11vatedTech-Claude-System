# 1. CINDERWORKS_ABBEY

## Concept thesis

A colossal monastic heat-engine once kept sacred fire alive. Theology and machinery became one civic-sacred system. The furnace-cathedral is dying; the player restores three coals by rejoining heat circulation through reliquary infrastructure.

## Emotional target

Awe under pressure. Player feels small inside a sacred public utility that outlived its monks. Success feels like restarting breath in a dying monument.

## Architectural language

- Gothic abbey massing fused with furnace, crematorium, aqueduct, and coal-refinery logic.
- Tall nave, rib vaults as heat channels, ash-settling basins, kiln chapels, bellows towers, chimney spires.
- Processional routes overlap maintenance-priest circulation: intake, balance, heart.
- Central furnace apse visible through grates, arches, cables, balcony cuts.

## Shape language

- Vertical ribs, long processional axes, arched compression, circular furnace throat.
- Thin gold seams against heavy black mass.
- Bell cables as nervous system.
- Gate wheels, ducts, shutters, grates, flues.

## Material palette

- Soot-stained limestone, glazed black brick, clinker brick, basalt, cooled slag.
- Heat-treated iron, blackened oak, tarnished brass, furnace ceramic, dirty glass.
- Accent: cracked gold leaf, coal orange, wax-dulled brass, brief white-hot pulse.

## Color script

1. Spawn: cool ash gray / blue skylight, minimal ember.
2. First reliquary: small amber intake pulse.
3. Mid-route: brass/gold line strengthens but stays local.
4. Hostile: hot red-white constriction and ash reversal.
5. Success: low furnace breath, gold ducts wake, distant village-flue hint.
6. Failure: warmth collapses inward; environment returns to cold ash.

## Value script

- Dark structural mass: walls, rib vaults, columns.
- Mid-value route: ash-settling floor, stairs, platforms.
- High-value focal: reliquary seam, furnace throat, route pulse.
- Avoid black crush in lower apse; floor edges remain readable.

## Lighting philosophy

Cold roof-wound key plus expensive low warm practicals. Warm light travels through ducts during timing windows. No uniform orange fill. Key/fill/rim sources must be physically visible: clerestory, furnace cracks, duct seams, candles, coal cores.

## Environmental story

The abbey worshipped sustained heat as communal mercy. It functioned as monastery, crematorium, coal refinery, and settlement heating plant. It failed through fuel scarcity, illegal duct taps, ritual exhaustion, and monks sacrificing everything burnable. The three coals separate because intake, balance, and heart systems stalled independently during final heat-cycle.

## Landmark system

- Primary: dead rose window over furnace apse.
- Secondary: intake kiln shutter, cloister cistern junction, bell cable line.
- Tertiary: gold seams, hand-polished valves, flue arrows, ash direction.

## Player path

Spawn balcony → Intake Reliquary / Ash Gate Kiln → cloister ambulatory or risky basin shortcut → Balance Reliquary / Cloister Cistern → lower crossing stairs → Heart Reliquary / Furnace Nave Apse → furnace restart / crypt descent hint.

## Reliquary relationship

Reliquaries are not collectibles. They are valves in a sacred heat network. Player attunes when heat, cable tremor, and seam alignment make the machine briefly safe.

## Safe / hostile state language

- Safe: widening gold seam, steady bell tremor, ash lifts away from route, warm duct thickness increases, low harmonic bell.
- Hostile: seam narrows/judders, cables snap tension, ash moves backward, furnace coughs, rim light becomes jagged, metal groans.

## Success transformation

Furnace breathes. Bell rings once. Ducts glow toward previous stations. Ash clears from a service loop. Distant village flue glows briefly. Space shows life support restored, not puzzle solved.

## VFX language

Sparse ash, duct pulse, ember leak, heat shimmer, upward ash during success. No constant ember spam.

## Audio environment language

Low furnace drone, cable tremor, bell resonance, valve grind, ash hush, duct cough. Success adds synchronized bell + low thermal inhale. Failure collapses into cough and dust fall.

## Anti-references

Generic lava temple, cathedral with orange cracks, floating crystal shrine, bonfire mimicry, clean Gothic kitbash, symmetrical boss arena, constant volumetric ember cloud.

## Signature opportunity

Black ash cathedral cut by thin sacramental gold veins; fire behaves like plumbing.

## Key study questions answered / still to prove

- Why abbey? Heat stewardship became theology.
- What worshipped? Sustained communal fire as mercy and continuity.
- How heat-engine worked? Reliquary coals fed flues through intake/balance/heart systems.
- Why failed? Fuel scarcity, illegal taps, ritual exhaustion, stalled cycle.
- Why coals separated? Different subsystems held partial heat memory.
- What changes on restoration? Ducts, bells, ash, flues, access loops.
- Identifiable motif? Soot-black sacred architecture with gold heat circulation seams.

# 2. EMBER_HOSPICE

## Concept thesis

Reliquaries are dying beings kept alive by sacred medical infrastructure. The player is not repairing machines; they are performing urgent palliative care on the last coals.

## Emotional target

Intimacy, vulnerability, dread, care. Success should feel like stabilizing a breathing patient. Failure should feel consequential and personal.

## Architectural language

- Monastery converted into thermal hospital and quarantine sanctuary.
- Ward corridors, intake rooms, surgical chapel, pressure doors, observation glass, pipe gantries, life-support engine hall.
- Devotional arches cut by curtain rails, altar converted to operating table, chapels subdivided into care rooms.
- Glass partitions reveal future objectives while communicating quarantine separation.

## Shape language

- Human-scale rectangles, cot rhythms, curtains, clamps, breathing tubes, domes.
- Circular gauges and coal lungs interrupt clinical grids.
- Surgical arms cast shadow glyphs.
- Reliquary cradles feel protective and restrictive.

## Material palette

- Off-white ceramic tile stained amber/gray, cracked enamel wall panels, warm concrete.
- Aged ritual metal, black iron rails, brass valves, burnt canvas curtains, rubber gaskets.
- Translucent life-support resin/glass, ash cloth, worn sacred surfaces.
- Accent: coal-red pulse, amber quarantine marks, phosphor green diagnostics, bone porcelain prayer masks.

## Color script

1. Spawn: cold failing lamps, muted tile.
2. Triage: soft coal-red pulse reads life.
3. Graft: amber quarantine and shadow surgery.
4. Engine: stacked red/gold life-support rhythm.
5. Hostile: distress light pulses irregularly; diagnostic green flickers.
6. Success: pulse stabilizes, amber relaxes, ward breathes warmer.

## Value script

- Mid-value clinical floors/walls must stay route-readable.
- Reliquary patient pulse becomes brightest local value but not global wash.
- Glass highlights controlled; no clipped partitions.
- Hostile readability comes from rhythm/shape/silhouette, not only red hue.

## Lighting philosophy

Surgical darkness. Cold lamps define institutional failure; coal-red pulses define life. Amber quarantine lights guide route/danger. Timing windows read as breath and pressure: tubes brighten, gauges tremble, coal lungs inflate, shadow glyphs align.

## Environmental story

Doctrine became emergency protocol. Prayer names became triage tags. Families watched warmth fade behind glass. Attendants kept coals alive with pumps, valves, incense respirators, prayer instruments. The final ward locked down mid-procedure.

## Landmark system

- Primary: life-support pipe-organ / coal lung engine.
- Secondary: intake cradle, surgical chapel glass dome, observation gantry.
- Tertiary: triage tags, pressure gauges, amber floor strips, breathing tubes.

## Player path

Spawn threshold → Triage Reliquary / Intake Ward → decontamination corridor → Graft Reliquary / Surgical Chapel → observation gantry → Choir Reliquary / Life-Support Engine → ward stabilization / sealed core opens.

## Reliquary relationship

Each reliquary is a patient with identity and support apparatus. Interaction is care under timing pressure. The player watches distress, safe breath, rejection, and stabilization.

## Safe / hostile state language

- Safe: pulse regularizes, cradle clamps relax, tubes brighten smoothly, breath interval opens, gauge needles align, soft choral monitor tone.
- Hostile: pulse stutters, tubes constrict/dim, curtain shadows jerk, pressure needles fall, coal lung collapses, alarm becomes organic distress.

## Success transformation

Ward lights stabilize. Life-support lung inflates. Pressure doors unlock. Glass clears. A patient tag flips from critical to stable. Audio shifts from wheeze to sustained tone.

## VFX language

Breath condensation, tube glow, steam purge, ash motes in lamp shafts, diagnostic flicker, surgical shadow alignment. No gore wall, no sci-fi hologram UI.

## Audio environment language

Heartbeat pulse, pump thump, soft flatline risk, valve hiss, distant ward rattles, prayer mask resonance. Success reverses flatline into breath. Failure makes distress audible, not merely loud.

## Anti-references

Dead Space corridor copy, modern hospital realism, random gore/tentacles, spotless lab futurism, generic blue holograms, hazard stripes as decoration, floating relic beds.

## Signature opportunity

Reliquary as patient. SAFE feels like heartbeat opening; HOSTILE feels like distress; success creates emotional attachment.

## Key study questions answered / still to prove

- Mechanic emotional strength: likely strongest of three, must be proven in first-room empathy test.
- SAFE as heartbeat: yes; use breath interval, tube widening, gauge alignment.
- HOSTILE as distress: yes; rhythm collapse, lung contraction, pressure fall.
- Failure consequence: strongest if audio/material/animation show patient decline.
- Could outperform Cinderworks emotionally: plausible; needs blind Founder and player evidence.

# 3. FALLEN_SUN_ORCHARD

## Concept thesis

Fire once behaved like life. Coals are seeds of extinct suns. The landscape is dying because its fire ecology collapsed; restoration can regenerate roots, color, heat, wind, pollen, weather, and false dawn.

## Emotional target

Mythic loneliness and renewal. Player feels they are crossing a dead ecology and briefly convincing the world to remember dawn.

## Architectural language

- Open-air megalithic orchard-temple, not interior shrine.
- Basalt terraces, root roads, tilted sun stones, petrified trunk reliquaries, collapsed observatory ribs, buried meridian vaults.
- Landscape anchors replace room props: split sun tree, collapsed bell causeway, buried solar colossus, eclipse horizon.
- Human pilgrimage additions wrap older cosmic/agricultural machinery.

## Shape language

- Long horizontal route bands, repeated dead rows, broken by towering organic/cosmic silhouettes.
- Root networks as route lines.
- Monolith shadow geometry.
- Circular seed/socket forms embedded in trunks/crown/bell.

## Material palette

- Volcanic soil, vitrified black wood, porous basalt, pale ash dunes, ash-coated stone.
- Charred bark, sun-baked sandstone, ceramic measurement tiles, weathered bronze mirror backs.
- Luminous seed material, heat-reactive organic surfaces, heat-glass shards.
- Accent: hammered gold inlay, ember pollen, false-dawn rim, copper-black eclipse sky, white-hot root pulse.

## Color script

1. Spawn: pale ash and copper-black eclipse.
2. Root: muted ember under black wood.
3. Causeway: wind-borne pollen glints and shadow rhythm.
4. Crown: gold/root convergence at high silhouette.
5. Success: local false dawn, restored color/heat/weather pocket.
6. Failure: pollen reverses, root glow splits into ghost-lines, horizon dims.

## Value script

- Sky and ash dunes hold broad mid-value field.
- Vitrified trunks/monoliths create dark silhouettes.
- Root pulse and reliquary sockets form controlled high-value path cues.
- Avoid open-field flatness by compression/release: rows, clearings, climb, vista.

## Lighting philosophy

Eclipse world. Broad dim sky preserves silhouette and loneliness. Fire appears as buried root glow, pollen motes, rim light, and rare local false dawns. Sunlight is mechanic, not beauty fill.

## Environmental story

A culture tried to cultivate replacement suns. War broke the canopy and meridian machinery. Survivors opened immature sun husks too early. Later ash-nomads reused sacred mirrors as shelter. The orchard is both farm, temple, observatory, and failed restoration system.

## Landmark system

- Primary: buried solar colossus crown.
- Secondary: split sun tree, collapsed bell causeway, observatory ribs.
- Tertiary: root glow, meridian lines, pollen direction, cairns, shadow markers.

## Player path

Spawn ridge → Root Reliquary / Split Sun Tree → lower cairn path or ash dune shortcut → Bell Reliquary / Collapsed Causeway → observatory rib climb → Crown Reliquary / Buried Solar Colossus → slide/drop to meridian gate.

## Reliquary relationship

Reliquaries are sun seeds grown into the landscape. Player attunes when roots, wind, shadow, and monolith align around seed sockets.

## Safe / hostile state language

- Safe: roots converge, pollen flows toward socket, shadow edge aligns, wind chime rhythm steadies, local warmth opens.
- Hostile: root lines split, pollen reverses, monolith shadow wobbles away, wind shears, ash veils path edges briefly.

## Success transformation

False dawn blooms locally. Roots glow outward. Dead flora lifts or regains ember color. Ash clears from meridian gate. Weather calms. Prior route becomes visible from high crown.

## VFX language

Ember pollen, ash wind volumes, root pulse under ash, heat shimmer at seed sockets, false-dawn rim. Must remain sparse enough for path/landmark readability.

## Audio environment language

Low eclipse wind, ash hiss, hollow bark creaks, buried root hum, stone bell swing, pollen whisper. Success introduces brief dawn chorus made from ember tones, not birdsong literalism.

## Anti-references

Generic orange fantasy forest, golden tree clone, glowing mushroom alien biome, shrine object placement, empty pretty ruins, flat desert with props, random tentacle flora, vista that hides mechanic.

## Signature opportunity

Fire as ecology: coals are seeds, roots, pollen, false dawns, and dead suns.

## Key study questions answered / still to prove

- Restoration can regenerate environment: strongest transformation potential.
- Stronger progression than interior chamber: plausible through color/flora/heat/weather restoration over distance.
- Performance/production implications: highest risk; terrain, fog, particles, instancing, long sightlines, and outdoor lighting need proof.
- PCG possibilities: strong for rows/debris/ash/vegetation, but must remain authored enough to avoid pattern noise.
