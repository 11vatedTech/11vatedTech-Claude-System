# Ashwake Freeze

Status: `CALIBRATION_FIXTURE_001`
Effective: 2026-08-22

Ashwake — The Last Reliquary has completed its calibration role. Its current
project, packaged state, captures, review evidence, content contracts, and
failure history are preserved as regression evidence.

## Allowed Future Changes

Ashwake may only be changed when a new global Foundry capability requires a
bounded regression fixture. Such a change must declare:

- the global capability under test
- the maximum scope and number of repair iterations
- the expected reusable lesson
- the regression evidence to be added
- the stop condition

## Prohibited Calibration Drift

Do not continue environment option selection, environment production, review
board polish, level expansion, broad gameplay work, audio polish, VFX polish,
or other Ashwake improvements for their own sake.

## Authority

Ashwake remains useful as a known-good engineering / known-weak creative
fixture. It is not evidence that the Foundry can produce a polished or
production-quality game. Human and independent review evidence remains
authoritative for creative quality claims.
